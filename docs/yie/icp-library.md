## California Food and Beverage Importers

This is the detailed reference ICP. Required evidence covers:

- active operations;
- food or beverage industry;
- California headquarters or meaningful operations;
- imports, foreign suppliers, international sourcing, or international distribution;
- meaningful B2B activity.

Preferred signals include 20–500 employees, wholesale/distribution/manufacturing/importing/brand-owner models, multiple suppliers and supplier countries, an identifiable finance function and decision maker, recurring supplier payments, and operational complexity beyond a local storefront.

Explicit exclusions include restaurants, local groceries, consumer-only storefronts, domestic-only microbusinesses, inactive companies, unverifiable non-operating brands, and businesses without meaningful international sourcing or payment need.

Recommended contacts include the CFO, Controller, VP Finance, Finance Director, Treasury Manager, AP Manager, COO, and Founder/CEO at smaller businesses. Sources include official sites, sourcing pages, catalogs, trade directories, regulatory/government registries where lawful, credible publications, job postings, expansion announcements, professional profiles, and permitted import records.

Unknown evidence is never silently converted into a match: each criterion explicitly chooses `FAIL`, `ALLOW`, or `REVIEW`.

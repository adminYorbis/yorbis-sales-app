# Yorbis Solution Knowledge Base

Pocket 3 introduces a persistent, reviewable definition of what Yorbis sells. The seed is business context, not model output. Every item has a stable definition ID and immutable numbered versions.

## Active solution profile

**Yorbis Global Payments Platform** connects pay-in and payout workflows for small and midsize businesses. It supports domestic and international fiat movement, global supplier and vendor payments, international collections, multi-currency and FX workflows, stablecoin-enabled capabilities where appropriate, card processing, provider orchestration, and consolidated payment visibility.

## Human review boundaries

The catalog does not assert geographic or currency availability, pricing, settlement speed, licensing status, guaranteed savings, or regulatory coverage. Those require separately approved product data. The four extra trigger definitions needed by the detailed California ICP—new product line, distribution expansion, warehouse expansion, and FDA/import activity—also remain hypotheses until evidenced for a prospect.
